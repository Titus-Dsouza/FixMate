# FixMate — Official Ranking Parameters

1. Verified Resolution Rate — 25%
   Measures the percentage of assigned complaints genuinely resolved and confirmed by citizens.

2. Resolution Within Deadline — 20%
   Measures how consistently an official resolves complaints within the applicable deadline.

3. Response Time — 15%
   Measures how quickly an official acknowledges complaints and takes the first meaningful action.

4. Overdue Complaint Management — 15%  
   Evaluates how effectively an official manages pending and overdue complaints, considering their age, severity, and workload.

5. Citizen Satisfaction — 10%  
   Measures citizen feedback about whether the reported issue was adequately addressed.

6. Evidence and Verification Quality — 10%  
   Evaluates the quality and reliability of evidence provided to support a claimed resolution.

7. Reopened Complaint Rate — 5%  
   Measures how often resolved complaints must be reopened because the original issue remains unresolved. Fewer substantiated reopenings result in a higher score.

Total: 100%

*Note: These are proposed weights for FixMate. Scores should be calculated from verified records using transparent rules, with AI helping analyze patterns and explain results.*
